# Owner requirement - independently selectable display units

**Product requirement approved by the owner, 2026-11-18:** users must be able to independently choose the display units for horsepower/power, torque, pressure and other vehicle statistics, including simultaneous metric and imperial values. This is **not** an instruction to replace SI physics. Exact TypeScript enum names, formatting precision and widget controls remain implementation details for the designated agent owners and must respect change-control rules.

## Required behavior

1. **No mutually exclusive global "metric/imperial mode".** An optional quick preset (Metric, US, custom) may initialize defaults, but every quantity category remains editable independently afterward. Changing power from kW to mechanical hp must not reset torque from N·m to lb-ft or pressure from kPa to psi.
2. **Per-stat overrides, even inside one category.** Preferences have defaults by physical quantity and optional overrides by stable field/telemetry metric ID, so boost can display psi while oil pressure displays bar and tire pressure displays kPa. Resolution order: explicit metric override, quantity default, documented safe fallback. Changing an individual gauge override does not alter other gauges or the category default.
3. **Suggested initial catalog (extensible with dimension-checked registry):** power W/kW/mechanical hp/metric PS; torque N·m/lb-ft/lb-in; pressure Pa/kPa/bar/psi (inHg for relevant vacuum measurements); temperature K/°C/°F; vehicle speed m/s/km/h/mph; distance m/km/mi; physical lengths mm/cm/m/in/ft; displacement cm³ (cc)/L/cu in; mass kg/lb; force N/lbf; airflow kg/s/g/s/lb/min; energy J/kJ/kWh (other validated units only when dimensionally appropriate); fuel volume L/US gal/Imperial gal; fuel economy L/100 km/US mpg/Imperial mpg with an explicitly correct reciprocal conversion. Some naturally dimensionless values (throttle %, mixture fractions) need no unit switch.
4. **Pressure semantics:** gauge vs absolute pressure is a *measurement/reference type*, not interchangeable with the unit. A boost gauge in psi(g) must never silently become an absolute psi(a) number; conversions need the declared ambient reference when converting between those pressure bases. Never silently confuse US and Imperial gallons or mechanical hp with metric PS.
5. **Versioned preference model:** a `DisplayUnitPreferences` record includes a schema version, quantity-default unit IDs and optional stable per-metric overrides. It is **user/workspace UI state**, independent of `VehicleDefinition`, engine parameters, authoritative physical state, source calibration and trained models. Save/reload using versioned IndexedDB and portable ZIP workspace snapshots; migrate/validate safely and recover corrupt/unknown unit IDs.
6. **Consistent display/input:** Builder, Drive, Settings, torque/power readouts, gauges, Diagnostics, graphs/axes/tooltips and floating widgets honor resolved preferences. Editable numeric inputs convert at the boundary and are range-checked in SI; display rounding must not accumulate during repeated switching. Exported physics traces and presets retain canonical SI units. An explicitly requested presentation export may carry a separate annotated display-unit preference map without changing the raw physical data or their hashes.
7. **Accessibility and tests:** labels always show the unit and, where relevant, pressure basis. Test mixed units simultaneously, independent changes, same-category per-metric overrides, SI conversion/roundtrip and tolerance, correct gauge/absolute handling, stored preference and ZIP restoration, unknown units, and identical physics/events before and after changing display units.

## Ownership and gates

- **AG-M0-06:** quantity/field display-unit capability metadata and validated conversion boundary; **AG-M0-11a:** stable channel metadata for per-metric overrides.
- **AG-M0-18:** persistent versioned `DisplayUnitPreferences`, migration and recovery, separate from engine configuration.
- **AG-M3-04 / AG-M3-07 / AG-M3-08a:** Builder, Drive and telemetry/graph unit selectors and live displays.
- **AG-M3-11:** shared Settings editor, automatic persistence and ZIP restore. Future providers register additional compatible physical quantities instead of adding global-mode conditionals.
- Do not mark any existing AG/roadmap item complete from this planning change. Any subsequent shared public TypeScript contract change still requires the documented owner approval/ADR process.

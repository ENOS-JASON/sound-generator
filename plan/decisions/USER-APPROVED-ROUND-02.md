## Explicit non-decisions and remaining work

- **ADR-001 remains OPEN:** benchmark equally capable AudioWorklet-owned and Worker-owned real-time physics; then present results for **owner approval**. Do not infer a winner from browser support choice.
- The I4's two alternate controller configurations are approved; **the initial default preset** and engine dimensions/gearing are not.
- A valid computer-generated audio recording exported for a diagnostic replay is not a source recording: **the no-recorded-playback rule applies to the live synthesized car-sound engine**.
- Browser ZIP is approved at the *format family* level; Parquet/HDF5 research outputs are optional; exact codec/chunk format and library remain an ADR design.
- Windows/Linux and three desktop browser families are target platforms. Tests may be blocked on unavailable equipment but cannot claim unsupported platform success; document gaps honestly.
- The technical integrator's identity/mechanism and minimum automated merge gates remain to be specified.
- Audio source/mix loudness and spatial listener, default I4 selection, performance budgets, measurement provenance and offline experiment management are deliberately left for Round 3.

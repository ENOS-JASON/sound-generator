# 04 — 3D visualization and tuning UI

## 3D modes
1. Whole vehicle: orbit/zoom, hood open/exploded mode, drivetrain/wheels and tailpipe positions; camera positions by cabin, engine bay, roadside and exhaust.
2. Engine cutaway: animate crankshaft, connecting rods, pistons, cams/valves, throttle and starter from shared crank angle; show cylinder combustion pulses in firing order.
3. Diagnostic overlay: highlighted active components, spark/fuel cut cylinders, mass-air/torque/load paths, wheel slip and instantaneous audio-source positions.
4. Performance fallback: low-polygon procedural mesh, static fallback/2D controls when WebGL is absent; don't block audio.

## Technology-aware 2D/3D content

The required 2D Canvas cutaway shows a **complete horizontal side-view row for every cylinder bank** (inline, V or flat), with each piston/rod/crank, intake and exhaust valves, four-stroke phase, combustion, spark/fuel state and directional modeled intake/exhaust flow. Use bank metadata rather than odd/even assumptions. View switching to 2D or telemetry must suspend unused 3D rendering without interrupting sound or simulation; see [13](13-2d-engine-cutaway.md).

Technology-specific controls/overlays are composed from capability manifests: cylinder layout/firing order; mechanical choke/distributor and no-ECU mode; ECU map editor and tune strategy; intake filter/runner/ITB/compressor graph; battery/voltage/starter wear; gear/transfer case/diff lock; powertrain and tire forces. Unsupported controls are disclosed and disabled, not silently simulated. Include measured crank/wheel torque/HP/kW and loss breakdown with explicit measurement mode, per [20](20-torque-power-load-and-energy.md).

## Guided, capability-aware vehicle builder

The primary setup experience is **a multi-step wizard**, not one oversized universal form. Follow template/powertrain family, cylinder layout, **bore and stroke with automatically derived displacement**, fuel/ignition/ECU (or no ECU), intake/induction, exhaust/cooling, battery/starter, transmission, FWD/RWD/AWD/4WD transfer/differentials, wheel/load settings, audio/visual, optional advanced reference training and final review. Fields, allowed values, defaults and step visibility are dynamically generated by the selected component capabilities and physical constraints. Downstream incompatible choices must be explained before reset; maintain valid edits, drafts and undo. See [22](22-guided-vehicle-configurator.md).

Training setup has selectable Quick/Balanced/Detailed/Research fidelity and per-component zone/mesh controls. It shows solver-supported fields only (e.g., combustion gas zones, liner wall bands and layers, exhaust pipe cell count) with units and estimated memory/trace size; an expensive reference run executes in a Worker or offline CLI rather than freezing audio or UI. See [21](21-adjustable-fidelity-multizone-reference-simulation.md).

## Interaction
Transport: key-on, ignition/start hold/release, kill engine; throttle and brake pedals, clutch, gear controls, load/dyno selection, automatic/replay mode. Tuning groups: engine geometry, torque and friction, ECU, air/fuel, exhaust, audio, drivetrain, environment. Each knob has units, validated bounds, reset, tooltip/physical meaning and live values. Expose preset version, seed and model version. Advanced controls behind an expert toggle.

## Visualization consistency
- 3D observes interpolated authoritative angle, cylinder state and wheel speed; do not approximate combustion from visual clock.
- Slider values timestamped and sent to solver with smoothing; visual event highlights follow confirmed engine event stream.
- Render scales to viewport and quality preferences; avoid expensive shadows by default.
- Keyboard accessibility and non-3D diagnostics are required. Motion reduction disables camera effects, not physics.

## Editors/diagnostics
Graphs for RPM, torque, manifold pressure, engine temperature, wheel slip, audio spectrum, DSP load and event timeline; export logs for fitting/bug reproduction. Scenario editor displays waiting gate, timeouts and assertions. Training view compares reference-versus-fast model over held-out scenarios and exposes error/coverage. Autosave locally; file imports must be validated and size-limited.

## Deliverable gates
Running engine visuals follow timing and cylinder order; disabled graphics leaves engine/audio unaffected; responsive controls never inject audible discontinuities or drop worklet deadlines.
